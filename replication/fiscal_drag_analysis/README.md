# Fiscal Drag Analysis

This directory contains the Stata replication pipeline for the empirical analysis of fiscal drag in the Italian personal income tax system (IRPEF) over the 2020–2024 tax years.

The analysis combines administrative tax-return statistics from the Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF), historical IRPEF tax schedules and the ISTAT NIC consumer price index.

## Replication

The complete empirical workflow can be executed from Stata by running:

```stata
do "00_master.do"
```

The master script sequentially:

1. imports and prepares the MEF administrative tax data;
2. cleans and combines the annual datasets;
3. reconstructs statutory IRPEF liabilities and the inflation-indexed counterfactual;
4. calculates and aggregates fiscal drag;
5. constructs the marginal and cumulative fiscal-drag measures;
6. runs the triggered-indexation policy simulation.

### `raw_data/`
Contains archived MEF administrative IRPEF data for tax years 2020–2024.

### `main_outputs/`
Contains the principal figures and tables generated by the empirical analysis and reported in the paper.

## Methodology

For each income cell and tax year, the analysis compares the effective average tax rate generated by the actual statutory IRPEF schedule with a counterfactual schedule based on the 2020 tax structure whose thresholds are indexed to cumulative inflation.

The cell-level measure is:

$$
FD_t(Y) = ETR^{actual}_t(Y) - ETR^{indexed}_t(Y)
$$

Results are aggregated using the number of taxpayers in each income cell.

The analysis uses 2020 as the base year for the counterfactual indexation exercise.

## Data Sources

- **Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF)** — administrative IRPEF statistics
- **ISTAT** — NIC consumer price index
- **Historical statutory IRPEF tax schedules**
